STUDIO 3.0 - free music maker with built-in Auto-Tune
=====================================================

INSTALL
  Easiest: double-click Studio-Setup.exe. It installs Studio for your user
  (no admin needed), adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts, and makes .studio
  project files open in Studio when you double-click them.
  Portable: or just run Studio.exe from the "Studio" folder (keep
  resources.neu next to it).
  If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click
  "More info" -> "Run anyway" (it does that for any unsigned app).
  Needs Windows 10 or 11. Allow microphone / MIDI access when asked.

NEW IN 3.0 - FOR FL STUDIO USERS
  - Sampler / Slicer: load any sample and play it across the keyboard, or
    chop a loop into slices (auto-detects the beats, or chop 4/8/16/32).
    "Make pattern" writes the slices back as notes so you can rearrange them.
    Right-click an audio clip > "Chop to Slicer" works too.
  - 808 glides: turn on Mono / slide in the synth (or use the "808 Slide"
    preset) and overlap notes in the piano roll to slide between them.
  - FM synthesis (FM Bell, FM E-Piano, FM Bass, FM Growl presets).
  - Mixer buses: "+ Bus" in the Mixer, then set a track's Out to the bus to
    group tracks (all drums, all vocals...) and process them together.
  - True sidechain compressor: add "Sidechain Comp" to your bass/pad and
    pick the kick/drum track as the key - pro pumping.
  - Piano roll: chord stamps (click once = whole chord, in key or by type),
    Scale lock, ghost notes from other tracks, Tools menu (strum, humanize,
    legato, reverse, double/half speed, snap to key), Ctrl+B duplicate,
    Ctrl+Q quantize.
  - My samples: add your own sample-pack folders in the Browser, search,
    preview, and send sounds to the song, the Channel Rack or the Sampler -
    or drag them straight onto the timeline.
  - Audio tools (right-click an audio clip): Reverse, Normalize, Transpose
    (+/- semitones, keeps length), Chop to Slicer. Track menu: Bounce to
    audio (freeze). Drum clips: Make unique.
  - PAT / SONG button: PAT loops just the pattern or clip you're editing,
    like FL Studio.
  - FL-style keys: F5 hide/show bottom panel, F6 Channel Rack, F7 Piano Roll,
    F8 Browser, F9 Mixer, F10 Audio settings, F11 Effects, F12 Vocal Studio,
    Ctrl+R export.
  - Time signatures (2/4 to 7/4) and song markers (right-click the bar
    numbers: add marker, loop a section).
  - Channel Rack patterns can be 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 or 64 steps.

  NOT POSSIBLE IN STUDIO (vs. FL Studio): loading VST/third-party plugins,
  ASIO drivers, and FL's huge factory sound library.

NEW IN 2.1 - INPUTS & GUITAR AMPS
  - Choose the input for every audio track: built-in mic, USB mic, or any
    input of an audio interface (Input 1 / Input 2 / mono / stereo), with
    input gain and a level meter that tells you if you're too quiet or clipping.
  - Monitor (headphone button): hear yourself live through the track's
    amp and effects while you play or sing.
  - Guitar amp simulator: 6 amp models (Clean, Crunch, Lead, Metal, Fuzz,
    Bass), speaker cabinets (1x12, 2x12, 4x12, 4x10 bass, DI), noise gate,
    tremolo - plus all the other effects as pedals.
  - One-click sounds: Clean Sparkle, Funk Clean, Blues Crunch, Rock Lead,
    Metal, Fuzz Stoner, Surf Tremolo, Ambient Wash, Bass Amp, Acoustic,
    and vocal chains (Pop, Rap, Radio, Dreamy, Podcast).
  - Built-in guitar/bass tuner.
  - Audio settings (gear button): output device, default input, latency.
  HOW TO PLAY GUITAR INTO STUDIO
    1. Plug the guitar into a USB audio interface (or a USB guitar cable).
       A laptop's headphone/mic jack usually won't work well for guitar.
    2. Click "+ Guitar", pick your interface and the input you plugged into.
    3. Press "Monitor" (wear headphones), pick a sound, and play.
    4. Press R to record.

WHAT'S NEW IN 2.0
  PRO SOUNDS
  - 12 real recorded instruments: Grand Piano, Acoustic/Electric/Nylon
    Guitar, Electric Bass, Violin, Cello, Flute, Saxophone, Trumpet, Harp,
    Xylophone.
  - 32 synth presets (leads, pads, basses, keys, plucks, FX) and a full
    synth editor: 2 oscillators with unison, sub, noise, filter + envelope,
    amp envelope, LFO, pitch envelope. Save your own presets.
  - Arpeggiator (up/down/up-down/random/chord, 1-3 octaves).
  - 6 drum kits (Classic, 808, 909, Trap, Lo-fi, House), 12 drum sounds,
    per-sound pitch, swing, and trap hi-hat rolls (right-click a step).
  - Browser tab: 15 drum grooves, 10 chord progressions, bass lines and
    melodies that automatically follow your song key. Click to preview,
    one click to add.
  PRO MIXING
  - Effects on every track: EQ (drag the curve), Compressor, Saturation,
    Filter (with tempo wobble), Chorus, Phaser, Doubler, De-esser, Delay
    (ping-pong, tempo-synced), Reverb (Room/Hall/Plate/Cathedral/Spring),
    Lo-fi, Stereo Width, Sidechain Pump, Limiter.
  - Automation lanes: press "A" on a track, pick volume, pan or an effect
    knob, and click points onto the lane.
  - Mastering presets on the Master track (Loud & Punchy, Warm, Bright &
    Wide, Lo-fi Tape, Clean).
  VOCAL STUDIO
  - New, more natural Auto-Tune engine (keeps your voice's character).
  - Voice "Character" (deeper/lighter tone) and Pitch shift knobs.
  - Harmonizer: up to 3 automatic backing voices in key.
  - Voice styles: Cher, T-Pain, Kanye 808s / Stronger, Daft Punk Robot,
    Choir Stack, Octave Doubler, Chipmunk, Deep Monster and more.
  - Pitch Editor: see every sung note, drag notes to new pitches,
    "Correct all to key", flatten wobble.
  - Takes: turn on Loop and record - every pass becomes a take; pick the
    best one in the Takes tab.
  PRO WORKFLOW
  - Plug in a MIDI keyboard and play; arm an instrument track (red dot)
    and press R to record MIDI (loop on = overdub).
  - Piano roll: velocity lane, quantize, snap (1/32 to 1/4, triplets, off),
    box-select (Ctrl+drag), copy/paste, arrow keys to transpose/move.
  - Timeline: box-select, copy/paste, split (Ctrl+T), trim clip edges,
    audio fades (drag the top corners), clip volume, right-click menus,
    drag & drop audio files.
  - Audio can follow the song tempo (right-click clip > Follow song tempo).
  - Autosave + crash recovery, "save changes?" when closing.
  - Export WAV, MP3, or stems (one WAV per track).
  - Resizable bottom panel.

SHORTCUTS
  Space play/stop | R record | L loop | M metronome | Home go to start
  A W S E D F T G Y H U J K   play the selected instrument (Keys button)
  Z / X octave down/up | 1-9 drum sounds (drum track selected)
  Ctrl+Z undo | Ctrl+Y redo | Ctrl+S save | Ctrl+O open | Ctrl+E export
  Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V copy / cut / paste | Ctrl+D duplicate
  Ctrl+T split at playhead | Ctrl+A select all | Delete remove
  + / - or Ctrl+wheel zoom the timeline
